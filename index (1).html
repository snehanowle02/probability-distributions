Probability Mass Function (PMF)

Exact
P(X = k) = C(n,k) · pk · (1-p)n-k
P(X = 7) = C(20, 7) · (0.35)7 · (0.65)13
P(X = 7) = 0.1844
Meaning: The probability of obtaining exactly 7 successes in 20 trials with success probability 0.35 is 0.1844 (18.44%).

Cumulative & Complementary CDF

Cumulative
P(X ≤ 7) 0.6010
P(X > 7) = 1 − P(X ≤ k) 0.3990

Explanation: P(X ≤ k) means the probability of getting k or fewer successes.

In this setup, P(X ≤ 7) is 0.6010, while P(X > 7) is 0.3990.

Skewness ($\gamma_1$) $(2-p)/\sqrt{1-p}$

Probability Mass Function (PMF)

Exact
P(X = k) = (1-p)k-1 · p
P(X = 4) = (0.75)3 · 0.25
P(X = 4) = 0.1055
Meaning: The probability that the first success occurs exactly on trial 4, with success probability 0.25, is 0.1055 (10.55%).

Cumulative & Complementary CDF

Cumulative
P(X ≤ k) = 1 − (1-p)k
P(X ≤ 4) 0.6836
P(X > 4) = (1 − p)k 0.3164

Explanation: P(X ≤ k) is the probability that the first success happens within the first k trials.

In this setup, P(X ≤ 4) is 0.6836, while P(X > 4) is 0.3164.

Memoryless property: $P(X > m + n \mid X > m) = P(X > n)$. Having already failed $m$ times does not change the chance of the next $n$ trials all failing.
